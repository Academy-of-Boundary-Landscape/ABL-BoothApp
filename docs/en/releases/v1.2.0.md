# BoothKernel v1.2.0

> v1.1 made BoothKernel *usable*; v1.2 makes it **keep proper accounts**.
> This release rewrites the ledger: every item and every yuan has a source and a destination. Bundle deals, refunds, gifts and scrap, closing stocktakes and consignment payouts are all built on the new ledger.

:::warning Read before upgrading: old events and orders are not migrated
Your Product library comes over automatically, but v1.1's **events, orders and stock won't appear in v1.2**. They're kept intact in a backup file,
and the first time you log in to the Admin console after upgrading you can export them to Excel in one click. **Don't upgrade in the middle of an event.**
See [Upgrading from v1.1 to v1.2](/en/guide/upgrade-v1.2).
:::

## Highlights

### 📒 New ledger: goods and money are both double-entry

Stock used to be just a number: minus one on a sale, plus one on a cancel, and when it went wrong there was no way to find out why.
Now every order, payment, refund, gift and stocktake is a **balanced debit/credit entry**: which location goods moved from and to, which channel money came through and which circle it belongs to, nothing left out.

- When taking payment you pick a **Payment channel** (WeChat / Alipay / Cash / custom), and at closing you reconcile by channel
- "Complete order" is still **only a record**; it doesn't mean the system confirmed the payment arrived. That hasn't changed

### 🎁 Bundles & discounts

- Set up **bundles** per event: "any 3 postcards for 20" (duplicates allowed) or "new book + standee together for 50" (one of each)
- The customer's cart **automatically finds the cheapest combination** and shows "Applied: XX bundle −¥10"
- At checkout the vendor can **remove bundles one by one** and directly change the **Amount received** (round down or add a surcharge); the difference is recorded as a manual discount and doesn't affect consignment circles' share
- While setting up a bundle, a live preview shows the most / least a customer could get, so you don't configure it wrong
- Replaces v1.1's "negative-price product as a discount" trick

See [Bundles & Discounts](/en/guide/lots).

### 🧾 Closing & settlement

- New **closing wizard** in the Vendor view: clear orders → stocktake → take back → settle; quitting halfway keeps your progress
- **Settlement sheet**: where each circle's goods went, the net amount, and how much I owe whom, all in one table; exports to an Excel file with four sheets
- **Consignment payouts**: products belong to a circle, and goods you sell for others are settled separately automatically
- **Refunds** (line by line, partial refunds, different refund channel), **gift / scrap** records, **advances**, **settlement adjustments**, **payment recount**
- After settling the books are frozen; only advances, settlement adjustments and payment recount can still be added at home

See [Closing & Settlement](/en/guide/closing).

### ▦ Barcode scanning

- On the Customer order page, **scan barcodes continuously with the camera** to add items; works with commercial barcodes (JAN / ISBN) and code labels you stick on yourself
- Supports USB / Bluetooth **barcode scanners**, plug and play
- The product form can **scan to fill** the barcode field
- Fully offline, no network services involved

See [Barcode Scanning](/en/guide/barcode-scan).

### 🧭 Redesigned Admin console and Vendor view

- **Event workbench**: open an event and there's a status bar at the top, "Preparing → In progress → Settled", with tabs grouped into Pre-event / On-site / Closing
- **Pre-event prep**: "Pick from Product library" to list items in bulk; "**Import from previous event**" to bring over products, prices and bundles in one go
- The sidebar is now Events / Product library / Circles / Settings / Tutorial; the old "Dashboard" and "About" pages are merged into Settings
- The **Vendor view** now has three tabs, Orders / Inventory / Closing, shown as a bottom tab bar on phones; switching tabs doesn't interrupt new-order alerts
- **Go to ordering** in the Vendor view and **Back to Vendor view** on the order page: one tap to switch when taking orders on a tablet
- Vendors can view this event's settlement sheet directly
- The order list shows refunded amounts and refunded quantities per line
- Unified components and colours across the app; the tablet order page and the phone Vendor view have both been re-laid out

## Fixes & improvements

- Fixed the app **crashing on launch** on Windows without the VC++ runtime: the installer now bundles it, and if the AI model fails to load only photo recognition is disabled, not the whole app
- On Android, the AI recognition "Auto" mode now runs on CPU (in testing, NNAPI was actually slower for these models; you can still pick it manually in settings)
- Fixed **no alert sound for the first new order** when opening the booth with no pending orders
- Fixed Excel export failing from a LAN browser
- Fixed a batch of photo recognition issues: incremental indexing losing images, a failed rebuild still showing "Done", no index built after importing a product pack, and tall album photos being stretched
- When recognition is unavailable, the "Photo recognition" entry on the order page is greyed out instead of erroring after you tap it
- Fixed the camera light staying on after closing the camera (camera not released)
- Hardened `.boothpack` import: entries that would write outside the data directory are rejected
- The Admin console keeps reminding you to change the factory default passwords (`admin123` / `vendor123`)
- All UI text is now Chinese (some input placeholders used to be the English "Please Input")
- The database is snapshotted automatically before every upgrade, and restored automatically if the upgrade fails

## Upgrade notes

- **Windows**: v1.1.1 users can update in one click under **Settings → About & updates** (「设置 → 关于与更新」); v1.1.0 users need to download the installer manually
- **Android**: download the new APK and install over the old one. **Don't uninstall first** (uninstalling wipes your data)
- After the first launch, read [Upgrading from v1.1 to v1.2](/en/guide/upgrade-v1.2) to export old data and check circle ownership
- LAN access still uses port 5141 (HTTPS); certificates and QR codes work the same as before
