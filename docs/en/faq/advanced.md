# Advanced tips

Some tricks for things that don't need a dedicated feature.

## How do I give customers a discount?

Two ways, which cover almost every deal you'll offer at a booth:

**① Bundles**: in the Event workbench under **Pre-event · Bundles** (「展前 · 套装」), set up "pick any N from these items for ¥XX total". The customer's cart automatically applies the cheapest combination and shows how much they saved.

**② Flat price**: after tapping **Finish packing** (「完成配货」), change **Amount received** (「实收」) directly in the **Confirm payment** (「确认收款」) dialog. The difference is recorded as a Manual discount and charged entirely to Your circle; goods sold on Consignment for other Circles are still settled at their own prices. You can also raise it, e.g. to round up or when you threw in a small item that isn't in the system.

## Can I sell bundles or sets?

It depends on whether they can be split:

**Can be split** (5 books in one bag): set up a Bundle under **Pre-event · Bundles** (「展前 · 套装」). The books still count as 5 separate items, stock is deducted accurately, and customers can still buy them individually.

**Can't be split** (a shrink-wrapped gift box that's ruined once opened): add it as a separate product, with its own stock.

## How do I handle sold-out products?

When stock runs low or sells out, the customer page automatically greys out the product and blocks ordering it.  
You don't need to hide or remove it manually.

See [Bundles & discounts](/en/guide/lots).

## Can the next event reuse the previous event's setup?

Yes. In the new event's **Pre-event · Products** (「展前 · 商品」), tap **Import from previous event** (「从上一场导入」), tick the products and Bundles to reuse, and choose whether to keep the previous event's prices or use the current Product library prices.
Products and Bundles are imported together in one operation. If it fails, nothing is applied; you never end up with half an import.

## My products have barcodes. Can customers scan to add them?

Yes. Fill in the **Commercial barcode** (「商业条码」) for products in the Product library, or print the product code as a barcode label, and the Customer order page can add items by scanning with the camera or a barcode scanner. See [Barcode scanning](/en/guide/barcode-scan).
