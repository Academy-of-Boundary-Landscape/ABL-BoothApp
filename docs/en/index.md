features:
  - icon: 🚀
    title: Automated workflow
    details: Totals, stock deduction and reports are all automatic. No more tally marks and calculator punching.
  - icon: 📶
    title: Multi-device
    details: Take orders on a tablet, collect payment on a phone. One device runs the app; the others just scan a QR code and connect in the browser.
  - icon: 🛡️
    title: LAN architecture
    details: Built for messy convention networks. No internet needed — a phone hotspot is enough.
  - icon: 🔓
    title: Zero-setup deployment
    details: No server, no verification, no official account. Download, install, open, done.
  - icon: 🎁
    title: Bundles and deals
    details: '"Any 3 cards for ¥20", "New book + standee for ¥50" — the customer cart finds the cheapest combination automatically and shows how much was saved.'
  - icon: 🧾
    title: Closing and consignment settlement
    details: Close up in four steps — recount, stocktake, take back, settle. One settlement sheet shows what each circle gets and how much you owe whom.
  - icon: ▦
    title: Barcode scanning and photo recognition
    details: Scan barcodes with the camera or a barcode scanner to add to cart; for doujin goods without barcodes, just take a photo and it's recognized.
  - icon: 📒
    title: Every entry has a source and a destination
    details: From v1.2 the ledger is double-entry — goods and money always have a where-from and a where-to. Refunds, gifts, scrap and stocktake differences are all accounted for.
